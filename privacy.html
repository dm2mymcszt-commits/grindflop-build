Privacy policy

GrindFlop · last updated 30 October 2026

What the Google Drive sync uses

GrindFlop asks Google for one permission only: access to its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

What is stored, and where

  • The app's own data (settings and the app's records on your devices) is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before anything is uploaded.
  • The encrypted files go only into that app folder in your own Google Drive. The encryption key stays on your devices; Google cannot read the files.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's own secure credential store.

What is not done

  • No data is sent to any server run by the developer. There is none.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Google user data is used only to keep your own devices in sync, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • In the app, "Delete cloud sync data" removes the encrypted files from your Drive, and "Disconnect this device" removes the sign-in and key from that device.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
